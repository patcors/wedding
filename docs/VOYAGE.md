# The Voyage (Garden story, proof of concept)

The Garden's middle chapter becomes a voyage down the river that tells Patrick and Amelia's story, replacing the photo album ("A lifetime of little moments"). The opening (names, date) and the ending ("Meet us in the garden") stay. Status: proof of concept on branch `voyage-poc`. Nothing here ships until Amelia says so.

## The story in scenery

Two small sailboats travel downstream as the guest scrolls. Words are minimal: the only running text is the month and year, shown where the photo album used to be. Everything else is told by scenery.

- **Amelia's boat:** the wooden sailboat (`woodenSailboatGeometry.ts`) in its wooden colour with soft teal highlights.
- **Patrick's boat:** the same sailboat in soft yellow. (Other identifying features, not letters, come later.)
- **The opening:** under the title each boat is tied up at its own little jetty on the outer bank of its channel, Amelia's on the left and Patrick's on the right, lanterns still burning from the night. As the guest starts to scroll they let go their lines, ease their sails and pull out into their channels.
- **Before they meet (before July 2017):** the river is split by a small island and thick reeds. Each boat is in its own channel and can't see the other.
- **July 2017, they meet:** the channels rejoin and the boats travel side by side from then on.
- **February 2021 – March 2023 (Amelia in Sydney):** the river widens and the boats drift further apart, still in sight. They come back alongside in March 2023.
- **Company flags:** each small masthead flies a paper flag with the mark of whoever its sailor works for, and nothing before their internship. Both run up FinoComp's (its orange mark) as November 2018 shows and keep them into Sydney. As October 2022 shows (a placeholder until Amelia confirms the month) Amelia strikes hers and runs up a plain interim flag for her months tutoring and selling haircare online; as January 2023 shows she runs up HeirWealth's (its purple crown). Patrick keeps FinoComp's until March 2023, so for a while hers is HeirWealth's and his FinoComp's; he changes a little into March 2023's hold.
- **August 2024, they start dating:** the boats come together. Over the following months the hulls grow long and slim and deck planks are laid one by one between them, until just before their engagement they are one catamaran: two hulls and a shared deck.
- **April 2026, they get engaged:** the two small masts give way to one. Each boat furls its sail and brings its HeirWealth flag down, and its mast is lowered aft along its hull. While the date holds, one tall mast rises from the middle of the deck; its plain ivory sail unrolls, and a paper flag with their P&A monogram is run up to its masthead, flying to starboard. The catamaran sails on round the bend and docks under the one sail.
- **The wedding (April 2027):** the river makes an S-bend. Coming round it reveals a massive old tree (the old tree at Jasper's Berry) with a small jetty beneath it. The catamaran docks there.
- **Dawn to dusk:** the whole journey runs through one clear day, and the light never stands still. The opening is at soft dawn, warm low on the horizon under a pale blue sky; by the time the boats meet it is a bright, light-blue morning, clear blue at midday, a deeper blue through the afternoon, golden round the bend and dusk at the dock. The sun climbs and sinks and swings across the sky the whole way, so the shadows turn as they lengthen, and a light morning haze burns off by midday and gathers again at dusk.

**The balloon** (`eggs/Balloon.tsx`, `eggs/balloonGeometry.ts`): a teardrop envelope of twelve gores in faded terracotta, cream, ochre and dusty teal, with a terracotta crown, a deep teal skirt, a small wicker basket on four ropes and a burner that flickers now and then. ### Adding an egg

The contract is the comment at the top of `eggs/egg.ts`; in short:

- **Register:** add its id to `EggId` and set `egg` on its milestone in `journey.ts`, and map the id to a component in `eggs/index.tsx`. Each egg gets `EggProps`: its `milestone` and the `SlotProps`.
- **Time:** `eggTime(milestone, progress, reduced, settled)` is scroll since the date first showed, in milestone shares (`scrolledSince`): negative before, running through the date's hold. Arrive at 0; linger as long as the story allows, and leave before the dock.
- **Place:** the trees close over the river, so the only open sky is up its corridor, and portrait sees only about 17° either side of the middle. For something in the air, `inView(p, width, { distance, bearing, height, from, follow })` gives a spot relative to the camera (degrees right of the middle; `follow` below 1 lets it lag the camera's turn, as distant things do), `overRiver` keeps it over the water, clear of the trees, and `heightAt(distance, elevation)` turns degrees above the eye line into a height. Place it nearer than it looks and scale it down rather than truly far, where the canopies would hide it. Keep to the right of the top-left/top-centre, where the date label sits. Something on a bank can use a fixed world spot from `riverCenter`/`bankEdge` at `routeAtMonth(month)`.
- **Motion:** hold idle animation while `paused` (set for reduced motion too). Under `reduced` the camera cuts to each milestone's still, where `eggTime` returns `settled`: show the egg at rest there.
- **Budget:** one to three draw calls and well under 1,500 triangles, built once and disposed, no shadows, and `visible = false` outside its window. For comparison the scene draws about 2.7 to 4 million triangles a frame on desktop and 1.5 to 2.2 million in portrait, in 200 to 300 draw calls (pixel ratio 1, counting the shadow and water-reflection passes).
- **Light:** the sky changes from dawn to dusk, and the haze with it (`scene.fog`'s colour and start). Read colours from `scene.fog` each frame rather than fixing them, as the balloon does for its haze.

## How it is built

All in `src/components/garden/`.

- **`journey.ts`** is the single source of truth, pure and tested (`tests/journey.test.mjs`): `MILESTONES` (`id`, `month`, `description` for screen readers, optional `egg` and `room`), `DATES`, and the chapters over scroll progress p ∈ [0, 1]: arrival `[0, .05)`, voyage `[.05, .92)`, dock `[.92, 1]` (`ARRIVAL_END`, `VOYAGE_END`, `CHAPTERS`, `chapterAt`). The boats are moored from `DOCKED` (.97).
  - `storyMonth(p)`: story time in months since January 2017, piecewise-linear. Scroll is shared out by story, not calendar: each milestone gets one share (`ROOM` of progress) up to the next, with more for the meeting, Sydney, dating and the last stretch round the bend. Each date holds for a third of its share so the label can be read. `progressAt(month)` is the inverse (when a date first shows), `dateLabel(p)` gives "July 2017" during the voyage and `null` outside it.
  - `since(month, p)`: story months since a date (stands still while a date holds). `scrolledSince(month, p)`: scroll since a date in shares, which keeps running; use it for scenery tied to scroll.
  - `routeAt(p)`: where the boats are, as z along the river (x is `riverCenter(z)`). They drift from z 6 to 4 under the title, travel steadily to −164 through the voyage, and glide to the jetty at −172. `routeAtMonth(month)` is where they are on a date. 178 units in all.
  - `stillProgress(p)`: the reduced-motion still for p (the opening, the latest milestone reached, or the dock).
- **The launch jetties:** `launchJetties(width)` (`gardenGeometry.ts`) places one on each outer bank beside `ROUTE.start`, Amelia's a little shorter at the head; the further bank's reaches out further, so both heads are as far from the river's middle. Built in `voyageDock.ts` (`launchJettyGeometry`, its lanterns and `mooringLines`) at a smaller scale than the dock's jetty, Amelia's with a single bollard, a post and her lantern at the head's corner, capped in her teal, Patrick's with two bollards and his lantern back by the bank, capped in his yellow; drawn by `Launch` in `VoyageWorld.tsx`. Each boat lies alongside its jetty's head at p 0, stern to it and bow free (`berth` in `storyBoats.ts`); `castingOff(p)` lets the lines go at p .004 and brings the boats out to the middle of their channels by p .0425, sails easing out as they go (`berthed`), and the lanterns fade. Portrait's channels are only just wider than a boat beside a jetty, so there each jetty is a landing stage along the bank and its boat lies about mid-channel, setting off straight down it. Planting keeps off the jetties (`voyageClearing`); the first reed bed on the left bank stops just upstream of Amelia's, so it doesn't hide it; guests' boats bump off them (`launchJettyStems`, whose upstream sides slant so a boat glances off into the channel) and taps under them don't launch (`underLaunchJetty`).
- **`gardenGeometry.ts`** shapes the river. `VOYAGE` holds the scenery's positions (z falls downstream; `from` is upstream): the island (z 10 to just before the meeting at about −8.5), Sydney's widening (−57 to −68), the S-bend (−135 to −164, swinging the river 12 units to −x), the dock (z −172, jetty on the +x bank) and the old tree (z −175.5). `islandHalfWidth(z, width)` is the island's footprint and `channelOffset(z, width)` the middle of each channel beside it. `voyageLandmarks(width)` gives the jetty, the catamaran's mooring and the tree in world x/z. `RIVER_REACH` (z 26 to −260) is what's planted. Instances are batched by 20-unit stretch (`riverChunks`) and drawn only within the camera's reach (`useRiverReach`), so the longer river costs about what the old one did.
- **`JourneyProvider.tsx`** eases the scroll into `useJourney().progress` (the old damping) before any other frame callback; under reduced motion it jumps to `stillProgress`. Everything that moves with the story reads it in `useFrame`, so it moves with the camera, not the raw scroll. Slots get `SlotProps`: `paused` (guest pause or reduced motion: hold decorative animation), `reduced`, `mobile` and `width` (1, or `MOBILE_RIVER_WIDTH` on portrait).
- **`cameraRig.ts`** (pure, tested in `tests/cameraRig.test.mjs`) and **`VoyageCamera.tsx`**: the camera rides 12 behind the boats at the old height, looking 34 past them, turning through the bend only as far as keeps the boats within about 10° of the middle of the picture (6° in portrait). Over the start of the dock chapter it settles on `dockView`: over the moored catamaran's stern quarter, so the sail and the monogram's flag read beside the jetty, with the old tree to the right and its lights above the copy (portrait sits higher, with the boats below the copy's button). The camera's far plane now stops just past the fog.
- **`GardenScene.tsx`** is wiring only. The rest of the old file moved to `GardenWater.tsx` (water and guest-launched boats), `GardenBankDetails.tsx` and `GardenDrift.tsx` (petals and falling leaves).
- **The boats' rig** (`storyBoats.ts`, `storyBoatGeometry.ts`): `joining(p)` runs from the dating date to the engagement's, so the last plank is down just before April 2026 shows. `rigging(p, still)` then times the change of masts by scroll since that date, in shares (`RIG_CHANGE`): the small sails furl and their flags come down (−.3 to −.1), the small masts are lowered aft (−.2 to .04), the big mast rises (.04 to .34, while the date holds), its sail unrolls (.3 to .58) and the monogram's flag goes up it (.5 to .8), all before July 2026's balloon. Under reduced motion each still shows the rig settled: two masts before the engagement, one from it. The big mast (`BIG_RIG`) stands mid-deck and its plain sail is eased well out to port, away from the jetty, so its face turns to the camera behind and at the dock. Its flag (`LOGO_FLAG`, .52 × .38, larger than the company flags so the monogram reads at the dock on a phone, and short enough to stay clear of the copy beside it on desktop) is `public/logo.png` (ink on transparency) on paper, flying to starboard, clear of the sail.
- **The company flags** (`companyFlags(p, still)` in `storyBoats.ts`): `FLAG_CHANGES` lists each boat's changes in order, each striking the flag before and running the next up the same halyard over spans of scroll since its month, in shares. FinoComp's go up both over the first .3 of November 2018's share. Story months rush through Sydney (October 2022 to January 2023 is about a tenth of a share, January to March under a tenth), so Amelia's changes are quick, starting as each month shows: the interim flag is fully up for about .05 of a share, and her HeirWealth flag is fully up beside Patrick's FinoComp one from p .424 to .4295 (his strike .1 to .18 and hoist .18 to .3 of March 2023's share). Change Amelia's leaving month in `AMELIA_LEFT_FINOCOMP`. All are struck as the small sails furl at the engagement. A reduced-motion still shows each change settled from its month, so the stills show both FinoComp's (November 2018 to February 2021) or both HeirWealth's (March 2023 to January 2026); none falls on the interim months. Drawn in `storyBoatGeometry.ts` (`flagGeometry`, `FLAG` .36 × .26, `companyFlagTexture`): each mark is a small PNG on transparency in `public/textures/garden/`, centred on paper, named in `MARKS` in `VoyageBoats.tsx`: `finocomp-mark.png` (Amelia's supplied logo, `#f08805`) and `heirwealth-crown.png` (`#783593`), both 128px. The interim flag's mark is `null` (plain paper) until Amelia sends the tutoring and haircare logos: name one in `MARKS.interim`, or for two, add a company to `Company` and a change to Amelia's list in `FLAG_CHANGES`.
- The story boats are kinematic: their positions are a function of progress (`storyBoats.ts`). They don't use `boatPhysics.ts`, which stays for the guest-launched "Float a boat" boats. Those keep to the channels round the island (`islandHalfWidth` is the physics' `island` shore), and bump off the little jetties at the start and the jetty at the dock (`launchJettyStems`, `jettyStems`) and, once it is tied up, the moored catamaran (`mooredStems`). In portrait the channels at the island's head are narrower than a paper boat, which can catch there; "Float a boat" launches beside the island past its head.
- **Light:** `skyCycle.ts` runs the day (`skyAt`, tested in `tests/skyCycle.test.mjs`), and `GardenSky.tsx` applies it each frame to the sky dome, background, fog, sun, hemisphere light, water and the page's copy. Keyframes sit on milestones, at most a few milestones' scroll apart, and the sky moves in straight lines between them, so it never settles:

  The sun's azimuth swings about 150° from dawn to dusk while its elevation climbs to midday and falls. `haze` sets the fog's start between a clear day's (38, or 22 in portrait) and the old mist's (16, or 8); the far end is fixed. Each key's `waterLight` multiplies the river's own colour (a `WaterStyle`), so any water brightens in the morning, warms in the golden light and dims at dusk, and `glint` is the sun's sparkle on it. `lampsAt(p)` brings the festoons and the jetty's lantern up through the second half of the dusk, so they glow as the old tree comes round the bend.
- **Review panel (dev only):** *Sky* switches between the clear day and the Garden's old misty day (`MIST_KEYS`, unchanged, for comparison); it reloads, so Safari's bars and the cover open in that sky's colours. *Water* picks the river's colour from `WATERS`: deep blue (Patrick's clear-day water, the default), clear blue, teal, sage mist (the old river) and a dark mirror. Both are remembered in `localStorage` (`pa_dev_sky`, `pa_dev_water`).
- `Garden.tsx` maps its chapters to the journey's boundaries and `.garden-scroll-space` is 2000svh, about a screen per milestone. During the voyage the page shows only the date, high in the sky above the boats, and a quiet "The next chapter" link to the celebration. The date follows the scroll eased as the camera eases it, crossfades, and changes at most every 400ms while rolling. Screen readers hear each milestone's date and `description` from a polite status line once the guest settles on it (`milestoneAt`). The scroll cue and the ←/→ keys step through `STOPS`: the opening, each milestone halfway through its date's hold, and the celebration (`nextStop`, `previousStop`).
- Text over the sky takes its colours from custom properties GardenSky sets on `.garden-prototype`: `--garden-sky-glow` (the glows behind copy, a paler wash of the sky's own colour: light blue by day, warm at dawn, golden and dusk), `--garden-ink-on-sky` (dark ink, slate-blue by day, with softer tints mixed from it) and `--garden-sky-top` (the top wash and the phone status-bar band). The test keeps the ink at a contrast above 8.5 on its glow all day and the glow tinted like the horizon. `GardenPage.astro` opens the page in the dawn's colours (`--garden-page` for the page, cover and `theme-color`, and the three above), so the cover and opening copy match the scene before it arrives. Safari's bars keep the dawn's blue all the way down; the phone's top band starts in it and fades through the sky's top colour, so there is no seam.
- **Reduced motion** (the operating-system setting for people who get motion sickness): instead of freezing at the opening, the camera cuts to a still view at each milestone, with no glide and no decorative animation.
- Phones are a first-class target. Check every change at a 390 × 844 portrait size as well as desktop.
