# The Voyage (Garden story, proof of concept)

The Garden's middle chapter becomes a voyage down the river that tells Patrick and Amelia's story, replacing the photo album ("A lifetime of little moments"). The opening (names, date) and the ending ("Meet us in the garden") stay. Status: proof of concept on branch `voyage-poc`. Nothing here ships until Amelia says so.

## The story in scenery

Two small sailboats travel downstream as the guest scrolls. Words are minimal: the only running text is the month and year, shown where the photo album used to be. Everything else is told by scenery.

- **Amelia's boat:** a toy sloop (`BOAT_LOOK` in `boatLooks.ts`): a glossy soft teal hull over a white bottom, a little cream cabin, and a white main and jib striped in teal, the jib eased the other way to the main, wing on wing.
- **Patrick's boat:** the same toy sloop in soft yellow. (Other identifying features, not letters, come later.)
- **The opening:** under the title each boat is tied up at its own little jetty on the outer bank of its channel, Amelia's on the left and Patrick's on the right, lanterns still burning from the night. As the guest starts to scroll they let go their lines, ease their sails and pull out into their channels.
- **Before they meet (before July 2017):** the river is split by a small island and thick reeds. Each boat is in its own channel and can't see the other.
- **July 2017, they meet:** the channels rejoin and the boats travel side by side from then on.
- **February 2021 – March 2023 (Amelia in Sydney):** the river widens and the boats drift further apart, still in sight. They part at the nose of a low sandstone rock in the middle of the river, Amelia's on the left and Patrick's on the right, and as February 2021 shows Amelia's sails under a miniature Sydney Harbour Bridge, past the Opera House on the rock (see "Sydney"). They stay apart through Amelia's two changes of work and come back alongside in March 2023.
- **The flags fly at the sterns:** each boat's university and work flags fly from two small ensign staffs at its stern, one on each quarter, low and in front of the sails as the camera sees them from behind, so the masthead is left bare until the big mast flies their monogram. The work flag flies from the outboard staff (Amelia's to the left, Patrick's to the right) and the university's from the inboard one, towards the other boat, so when both fly they read side by side, each on its own staff. A staff stands only while it has a flag: it is put up just before its first flag goes up and taken down once its last comes down, so the sterns are bare under the title and at the dock.
- **The university's flag:** while they're at university, from the meeting to graduation, both boats fly the University of Wollongong's flag, its shield on paper, from the inboard staff. It is run up as July 2017 shows, flies beside FinoComp's from November 2018, and comes down as August 2020 shows, just before the mortarboards go up.
- **Company flags:** each boat flies a paper flag from its outboard staff with the mark of whoever its sailor works for, and nothing before their internship. Both run up FinoComp's (its orange mark) as November 2018 shows and keep them into Sydney. As August 2022 shows Amelia strikes hers and runs up a plain interim flag for her months tutoring and selling haircare online; as January 2023 shows she runs up HeirWealth's (its purple crown). Patrick keeps FinoComp's until March 2023, so for a while hers is HeirWealth's and his FinoComp's. Each of these is a milestone with time of its own, so the interim flag, and then the two different flags, each fly for most of a screen (about 87svh of scroll each); each change is done as the date settles.
- **August 2024, they start dating:** while the date holds the boats come together and the first two crossbeams go across between them, and butterflies fly out of the trees on Patrick's bank to circle them (see "The dating butterflies"), while on Amelia's his bird has just flown home, as July showed (see "The apartments"). Over the following months the hulls grow long and slim and deck planks are laid one by one between them, until just before their engagement they are one catamaran: two hulls and a shared deck.
- **January 2026, salsa classes:** as the date shows, the catamaran turns once on the spot like a dancer, everything aboard going round with it, and sails on.
- **April 2026, they get engaged:** the two small masts give way to one. Each boat furls its sail and brings its HeirWealth flag down, its stern staff is taken down, and its mast is lowered aft along its hull. While the date holds, one tall mast rises from the middle of the deck; its sail unrolls, the toy sloops' white cloth with each of their two stripes now a band of Amelia's teal under one of Patrick's yellow, and a paper flag with their P&A monogram is run up to its masthead, flying to starboard. On the deck a little man in Patrick's yellow goes down on one knee to a little woman in Amelia's teal and holds up a ring, then stands, and they ride on hand in hand (see "The proposal"). Rose petals trail behind it on the water from the engagement, a few fluttering down from the new sail as it unrolls, and ease off before the bend. The catamaran sails on round the bend and docks under the one sail.
- **The wedding (April 2027):** the river makes an S-bend. Coming round it reveals a massive old tree (the old tree at Jasper's Berry) with a small jetty beneath it. The catamaran docks there.
- **Dawn to dusk:** the whole journey runs through one clear day, and the light never stands still. The opening is at soft dawn, warm low on the horizon under a pale blue sky; by the time the boats meet it is a bright, light-blue morning, clear blue at midday, a deeper blue through the afternoon, golden round the bend and dusk at the dock. The sun climbs and sinks and swings across the sky the whole way, so the shadows turn as they lengthen, and a light morning haze burns off by midday and gathers again at dusk.

One skinned mesh (a bone for each joint and the ring), one draw call, 1,040 triangles, no shadows cast, hidden until they come out; drawn again in the water's reflection. Holds while paused; under reduced motion the engagement's still shows him kneeling, and every still after it the two of them standing together. Tests (`tests/proposal.test.mjs`) check the timing against the rig change, the real-time steps, the reduced-motion stills, that nobody sinks into the deck or floats off it in any pose, that they keep between the hulls, aft of the mast, clear of the sail and forward of the petals, the poses (his knee down and ring held up to her, then hand in hand), their size beside the mast, and the budget.

### Adding an egg

The contract is the comment at the top of `eggs/egg.ts`; in short:

- **Register:** add its id to `EggId` and set `egg` on its milestone in `journey.ts`, and map the id to a component in `eggs/index.tsx`. Each egg gets `EggProps`: its `milestone` and the `SlotProps`.
- **Time:** `eggTime(milestone, progress, reduced, settled)` is scroll since the date first showed, in milestone shares (`scrolledSince`): negative before, running through the date's hold. Arrive at 0; linger as long as the story allows, and leave before the dock.
- **Place:** the trees close over the river, so the only open sky is up its corridor, and portrait sees only about 17° either side of the middle. For something in the air, `inView(p, width, { distance, bearing, height, from, follow })` gives a spot relative to the camera (degrees right of the middle; `follow` below 1 lets it lag the camera's turn, as distant things do), `overRiver` keeps it over the water, clear of the trees, and `heightAt(distance, elevation)` turns degrees above the eye line into a height. Place it nearer than it looks and scale it down rather than truly far, where the canopies would hide it. Keep clear of the date label at the top centre; through the bend the only open sky is just left of it (see the balloons). Something on a bank can use a fixed world spot from `riverCenter`/`bankEdge` at `routeAtMonth(month)`.
- **Motion:** hold idle animation while `paused` (set for reduced motion too). Under `reduced` the camera cuts to each milestone's still, where `eggTime` returns `settled`: show the egg at rest there.
- **Budget:** one to three draw calls and well under 1,500 triangles, built once and disposed, no shadows, and `visible = false` outside its window. For comparison the scene draws about 2.7 to 4.05 million triangles a frame on desktop (1440 × 900) and 1.4 to 2.2 million in portrait (390 × 844), in 205 to 305 draw calls (counting the shadow and water-reflection passes; measured at every milestone's stop, every year's turn and the eggs between them in October 2026, after the 2400svh rebalance: the busiest are the meeting and dating's stop, 304 on desktop, and the engagement's petals, 4.05 million triangles).
- **Light:** the sky changes from dawn to dusk, and the haze with it (`scene.fog`'s colour and start). Read colours from `scene.fog` each frame rather than fixing them, as the balloons do for their haze.

## How it is built

All in `src/components/garden/`.

- **`journey.ts`** is the single source of truth, pure and tested (`tests/journey.test.mjs`): `MILESTONES` (`id`, `month`, `description` for screen readers, optional `egg`, `room` and `hold`), `DATES`, when Amelia left FinoComp (`AMELIA_LEFT_FINOCOMP`, August 2022) and the placeholder month she is to confirm for moving into Patrick's (`AMELIA_MOVED_IN`), and the chapters over scroll progress p ∈ [0, 1]: arrival `[0, .05)`, voyage `[.05, .92)`, dock `[.92, 1]` (`ARRIVAL_END`, `VOYAGE_END`, `CHAPTERS`, `chapterAt`). The boats are moored from `DOCKED` (.97).
  - `storyMonth(p)`: story time in months since January 2017, piecewise-linear. Scroll is shared out in shares (`ROOM` of progress, about .041, 98svh of scroll). Every year from 2018 to 2025 gets the same scroll, `YEAR` (1.5 shares, 148svh), so the Januaries, and with them the year markers, come evenly however the milestones fall (Amelia's rule). Inside a year each milestone takes its `room` at its date (by default `HOLD`, .35 of a share) and the rest of the year is spread evenly over its twelve months, so a busy year's months rush and a quiet year's roll by. Around them, the run-in (the opening's channels, 1.4 shares, the meeting's 1.4 and the first coast trip's .5) and the run from January 2026 (salsa's and the engagement's share each, and the trip's 3.4, enough that its date shows just before the bend starts, then the wedding's hold) keep lengths of their own: there the months take no scroll and each room runs up to the next date. That makes 21.2 shares. Each date holds for `HOLD` so the label can be read, or for that much of a room longer than a share (the meeting, the trip), or for its own `hold`. `progressAt(month)` is the inverse (when a date first shows), `dateLabel(p)` gives "July 2017" during the voyage and `null` outside it.
  - 2024 is the busiest year, its three dates sharing one year's scroll: May (`room` .43) and June (.58, holding .27 so its room runs on into July) and August (.45, holding .42, nearly all of it) each show for about 42svh, and July for about 16svh; January to April and September to December rush past in a few svh. They showed for about 31svh each when every year was a screen and a third and the page 2110svh; 60svh each, with years still even, would need a page of about 3,400svh; Amelia settled on about 42svh (October 2026), so the page stays at 2400svh. Graduation takes 1.1 shares of 2020 (see "The graduation"), so its date comes early in the year and January to July 2020 roll by quickly. Amelia's interim flag and her HeirWealth flag beside Patrick's FinoComp one each fly for about 87svh (see the company flags).
  - `since(month, p)`: story months since a date (stands still while a date holds). `scrolledSince(month, p)`: scroll since a date in shares, which keeps running; use it for scenery tied to scroll.
  - `routeAt(p)`: where the boats are, as z along the river (x is `riverCenter(z)`). They drift from z 6 to 4 under the title, travel steadily to −164 through the voyage, and glide to the jetty at −172. `routeAtMonth(month)` is where they are on a date. 178 units in all.
  - `stillProgress(p)`: the reduced-motion still for p (the opening, the latest milestone reached, or the dock).
- **The launch jetties:** `launchJetties(width)` (`gardenGeometry.ts`) places one on each outer bank beside `ROUTE.start`, Amelia's a little shorter at the head; the further bank's reaches out further, so both heads are as far from the river's middle.
