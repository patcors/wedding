# Garden ash trees

Generated with [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree) 1.1.0 by Daniel Greenheck, from its Ash Medium preset and bundled oak bark / ash foliage maps. The MIT notice is retained in [EZ-TREE-LICENSE.txt](./EZ-TREE-LICENSE.txt).

The wedding project supplies the seeds, crown adjustments, material colour treatment, separate distant variants, compression, placement, and runtime wind. Textures have been resized, converted to WebP, and the colour maps softened to suit the garden palette.

Regenerate from the repository root with `pnpm generate:garden-trees`. See `scripts/generate-garden-trees.mjs` and `docs/GARDEN_TREES.md` for the complete workflow.
