# Garden prototype assets

- `ground-color.jpg`, `ground-normal.jpg`, `ground-roughness.jpg`: **Rocky Terrain 02**, Amal Kumar / Poly Haven. 1K JPEG maps, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02 and https://polyhaven.com/license
- `water-normal.jpg`: Three.js r185 example `textures/waternormals.jpg`, obtained from https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r185/examples/textures/waternormals.jpg . Three.js MIT license is included as `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- `finocomp-mark.png`: FinoComp's power-symbol mark, from the logo Amelia supplied (400px on white), keyed to its orange on transparency at 128px (the original is kept out of the repo). Used on the boats' FinoComp flags, for where Patrick and Amelia worked.
- `heirwealth-crown.png`: HeirWealth's crown icon, reduced to 128px purple on transparency from the 2000px icon (the original is kept out of the repo). Used on the boats' HeirWealth flags, for where Patrick and Amelia work.
- `uow-shield.png`: the University of Wollongong's shield, cropped from its official logo (`logo.svg`, navy `#061B3E`, from the UOW brand site, https://www.uow.edu.au/brand/brand-system/logo/ , served at https://www.uow.edu.au/assets/logos/logo-svgs/logo.svg ), without the wordmark, at 128px tall on transparency. Used on the boats' university flags, for where Patrick and Amelia studied. The UOW Mathematics and Statistics Society's own logo couldn't be found: its UniClubs page (https://clubs.uow.edu.au/mathematics-and-statistics-society/) no longer loads and its site (uowmassoc.org) no longer resolves. UOW's marks are the university's property; this is a private wedding site.
- Tree, root, leaf, petal, rock and bank geometry and the bark texture are generated by this prototype. No Noomo models, textures or source code are included.
