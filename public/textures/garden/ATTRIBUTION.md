# Garden prototype assets

- `ground-color.jpg`, `ground-normal.jpg`, `ground-roughness.jpg`: **Rocky Terrain 02**, Amal Kumar / Poly Haven. 1K JPEG maps, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02 and https://polyhaven.com/license
- `water-normal.jpg`: Three.js r185 example `textures/waternormals.jpg`, obtained from https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r185/examples/textures/waternormals.jpg . Three.js MIT license is included as `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- `finocomp-mark.png`: FinoComp's power-symbol mark, redrawn as clean arcs and a disc measured from the logo Amelia supplied (400px on white, kept out of the repo), in its orange on transparency at 512px. Used on the boats' FinoComp flags, for where Patrick and Amelia worked.
- `heirwealth-crown.png`: HeirWealth's crown, cut from its square app icon (`logo_single.svg` in HeirWealth's web app, kept out of this repo) and coloured its purple on transparency at 512px. Used on the boats' HeirWealth flags, for where Patrick and Amelia work.
- `uow-shield.png`: the University of Wollongong's shield, cropped from its official logo (`logo.svg`, navy `#061B3E`, from the UOW brand site, https://www.uow.edu.au/brand/brand-system/logo/ , served at https://www.uow.edu.au/assets/logos/logo-svgs/logo.svg ), without the wordmark, at 512px tall on transparency, with paper filling its white parts so the shield is the whole flag. Used on the boats' university flags, for where Patrick and Amelia studied. The UOW Mathematics and Statistics Society's own logo couldn't be found: its UniClubs page (https://clubs.uow.edu.au/mathematics-and-statistics-society/) no longer loads and its site (uowmassoc.org) no longer resolves. UOW's marks are the university's property; this is a private wedding site.
- Tree, root, leaf, petal, rock and bank geometry and the bark texture are generated by this prototype. No Noomo models, textures or source code are included.
